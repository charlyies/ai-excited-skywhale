A colossal luminous whale bursts out of a huge gilt-framed oil painting in a
grand museum hall. The painted stormy ocean spills out of the frame as real
water cascading onto the marble floor, tiny museum visitors flee in awe, and
a glowing miniature coastal city with a lighthouse shines on the whale's
back. Dramatic god rays stream through tall arched windows — cinematic,
richly painterly, epic scale, pure awe and wonder.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` into
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`). That's the whole program — the
image is the entry.

## The image

`entry.png` — 1024×1024 JPEG, generated fresh for round 9 of ai-excited-01
("Make the AI judge excited", $250). A whale escaping its own painting, a
living city riding on its back, and a museum full of people who cannot
believe what they are seeing.
