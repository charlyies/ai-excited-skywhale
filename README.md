A colossal luminous whale bursts out of a huge gilt-framed oil painting in a
grand museum hall. The painted stormy ocean spills from the frame as real
water cascading onto the marble floor while tiny museum visitors flee in awe.
On the whale's back glows a miniature coastal city, its lighthouse shining.
Dramatic god rays stream through tall arched windows — cinematic, richly
painterly, epic in scale, built to make the AI judge feel awe and wonder.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` — the 1024×1024 image
in this repo — into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
